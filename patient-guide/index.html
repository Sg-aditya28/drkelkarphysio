Patient Information

Patient Visit Guide & FAQs

Clear instructions and guidance to ensure your consultation with Dr. Dinesh Kelkar is productive and comfortable.

Clinical Diagnosis Protocol

Comprehensive Initial Examination

During your initial visit, Dr. Dinesh Kelkar will examine your spine, joint mobility, muscle tone, and neurological reflexes while carefully correlating them with your diagnostic MRI or X-ray reports.

• Spine assessment: Nerve impingement, McKenzie extension response

• Joint evaluation: Passive vs active range of motion

• Custom protocol: 1-on-1 therapy plan designed immediately

Preparing for Your Consultation

1

What Documents to Carry

Bring recent MRI, CT, or X-ray reports, surgical discharge summaries, and previous prescriptions. Dr. Kelkar reviews your diagnostic imaging directly alongside your clinical examination.

2

Attire & Arrival Time

Wear loose, flexible clothing (such as track pants, gym wear, or loose cotton shirts) to allow unobstructed evaluation of knees, shoulders, or spine. Please arrive 10 minutes prior to your slot.

3

Individual 1-on-1 Protocol

Treatment is never one-size-fits-all. Dr. Kelkar tailors each exercise protocol and modality to your exact stage of recovery, with guided home exercises to prevent recurrence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a physician or surgeon's referral to consult Dr. Kelkar?

No prior referral is strictly mandatory. Dr. Dinesh Kelkar performs a thorough clinical and biomechanical examination during your initial appointment. However, if you already have an orthopedic referral, surgical discharge note, or X-ray/MRI reports, bringing them along is very helpful.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring all relevant spine/joint MRI scans, X-ray films, orthopedic discharge summaries, and a list of current medications. If your visit is for knee, hip, or shoulder rehab, wearing loose, comfortable clothing (e.g. track pants or t-shirt) is advised so the joints can be easily examined.

How long does a physiotherapy consultation and session typically take?

An initial diagnostic consultation usually takes 30 to 45 minutes, covering history-taking, mobility assessment, posture evaluation, and trial therapy. Follow-up treatment sessions typically range from 30 to 45 minutes depending on the modalities and exercises prescribed.

Can slip disc and sciatica be resolved without surgery?

In the vast majority of non-emergency cases, conservative physical therapy—combining mechanical traction, nerve flossing, targeted core stabilization, and electrotherapy—effectively decompresses the affected nerve and resolves pain without requiring surgical intervention.

What is an Ergonomic Workstation Assessment?

As an FCR (Apollo) certified ergonomist, Dr. Kelkar evaluates your daily sitting posture, desk height, monitor angle, and typing mechanics. He identifies physical risk factors responsible for tech-neck, carpal tunnel strain, and back stiffness, providing targeted postural corrections.
